iv_02 · knobs 1 + 5

5 [ regen ]
feedback · keep it low while learning
past 3:00

in shm

time · spread

iv_03 · knobs 6 + 7 · fig.01
7 [ spread ]
base delay time: from now to the last tap
6 [ time ]
wait before the first tap, as a share of spread
clocked
with tap or a clock, spread divides or multiplies it
cv on spread
an lfo here gives the big warbles and pitch bends

skew · angle

iv_04 · knobs 2 + 3 · fig.01 · fig.02
2 [ skew ]
tap spacing · noon even · left starts fast and slows · right starts slow and speeds up
3 [ angle ]
turns each tap around the stereo circle from the last one
angle 1:00
taps drift right, step by step, then wrap round to the left
angle far
extreme settings bounce taps all over the field

08

six patches

iv_08 · from the manual · abc · xyz
angry ducklim or dst · <
short, low sound into in 1 · regen fully up so the input ducks the delay
snap crackle popshm · =
a clock or trigger pattern into in 1 · different clocks into each in for width
bouncing balllim · >
short plucky sound in · move angle to throw the bounce around
reverse delaylim · <
anything in · crescendo plus skew reads as a reversed echo
shimmersshm · >
longer melodic sound · regen above noon for the pitch shift
80sdst · >
anything in · lfo for colour · cv into spread for wild warble
